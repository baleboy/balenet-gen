Every year my hometown, Lucca, hosts a comics and games convention that takes over the whole city and sees hundreds of thousands of visitors over five days. The event started 60 years ago in a much smaller scale, and grew together with the popularity of its subject.

The last time I was at Lucca Comics (there was no Games back then) was in the early 90s, and it was much different.

After I moved to Finland the event grew bigger and bigger, but I never visited because it came at an inconvenient time and I never felt it was worth the trip. This time I decided to go and see it at least once.

Even though I didn't know this when I bought the tickets, there were a few special attractions this year that I was interested about: Frank Miller, Go Nagai and the Gundam Base pop-up store.

But the real surprise was the cosplay.
